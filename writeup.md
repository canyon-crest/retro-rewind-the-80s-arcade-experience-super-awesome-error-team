## 💡 The Inspiration
Our game Hey-T is based on both Pac-Man (1980) and BurgerTime (1982). The game is set in a maze where the player has to avoid the enemies (like how Pac-Man avoids the ghosts). We were especially inspired by the bird’s-eye view of Pac-Man, so we applied that to our game where we could see all parts of the maze, our plate sprite, and the enemy sprites (the Big H and his duck). At the same time, the player tries to collect certain ingredients to get closer to winning the game (like in BurgerTime, except our game uses them to create recipes while BurgerTime uses them to fight enemies). In order to win the game, we try to collect all ingredients for all seven drinks before Big H and his duck catches us, like how we try to collect all pellets in Pac-Man.
